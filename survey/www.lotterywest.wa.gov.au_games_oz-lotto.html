OZ Lotto

Add a little glam to your Tuesdays when you play OZ Lotto. This exciting jackpotting game is drawn every Tuesday night, so bring out the fancy dips this Tuesday, sit in your comfy chair and pick your numbers.

OZ Lotto is a jackpotting game, which means if Division 1 is not won the prize will increase in the next draw. The Division 1 prize pool starts from $4 million. The Division 1 prize pool is shared amongst the total number of winners in that Division - which could be just one person who takes home the entire amount.

OZ Lotto also brings you special Prize Boost events which boost Division 2-7 winners prize amount! Keep an eye out for these Prize Boost events as they happen throughout the year! Who doesnât love an extra boost to their Tuesday?

How could I win?

  • OZ Lotto is played by drawing ten numbers from a barrel of 47
  • The first seven numbers drawn are the Winning Numbers
  • The last three numbers drawn are the Supplementary Numbers
  • The more Winning Numbers you have in each game line, the bigger the prize
  • The minimum entry is one game
  • Overall odds are the chance of winning any of the prize divisions in a game. Division 1 odds refers to your chance of winning the top prize in the game.
  • If you buy a lottery ticket you are participating in a game of chance. The odds of winning are based on 1 standard game and are the same for each game played.
